Six platforms

SGT MOSFET

Mid and low voltage

A shield electrode in the trench balances gate charge and on-resistance, for efficient conversion and high-current switching.

DC-DC conversion, motor drives, motherboard and GPU power stages.

Trench MOSFET

Low voltage

The base platform: a dense trench gate for low on-resistance in a small die.

Conversion, load switches and general power stages.

CSP MOSFET

Wafer-level package

The die is the package. Wafer-level packaging keeps the footprint and the path resistance as small as the silicon itself.

Battery protection, charge and discharge paths in portables.

Super junction MOSFET

650–700 V

Alternating p and n pillars carry the voltage, for lower on-resistance in a given die area.

PFC, resonant and energy-conversion stages.

FS-trench IGBT

650 V

A field-stop layer under a trench gate balances VCE(sat) against switching speed.

Variable-frequency drives, UPS and charging.

DrMOS & PMIC

Smart power stage

Driver, high-side and low-side MOSFETs in one package raise power density and simplify multiphase layout.

High-current core rails on CPUs, GPUs and servers.

Where they work

Motherboard & GPU

Compute · SGT MOSFET, DrMOS

Vcore, memory and I/O rails on motherboards and graphics cards draw high current from tight board space. Low-resistance SGT MOSFETs and DrMOS power stages carry the key phases of these high-density compute platforms.

Power adapter

Consumer · Super junction, SGT MOSFET

In chargers and adapters, the primary side switches mains-derived voltage and the secondary side rectifies it down to USB levels. High-voltage super-junction parts suit the PFC and primary stages; low-voltage SGT parts suit synchronous rectification.

Power adapter parts

Motor drive

Industrial · SGT MOSFET, IGBT

Power tools, fans and general motor drives switch every phase of the motor many thousands of times a second. Gostone's low-voltage SGT MOSFETs are selected for these bridges, and FS-trench IGBTs for higher-power drives.

Automotive aftermarket

Automotive · Trench, SGT MOSFET

Add-on electronics in the car run from the 12 V system: in-car chargers, dash cameras, inverters and lighting. They need compact switches that cope with a wide supply range and a hot cabin.

Automotive aftermarket parts

Drone

Consumer · SGT MOSFET, CSP

Each arm of a drone carries a motor controller, and every gram and every degree counts. Low-resistance SGT MOSFETs keep the motor bridges cool, and CSP parts protect the battery in the smallest footprint.
